How amounts are chosen

Divides the entered amount across up to three games, weighting each selected game by its estimated return per dollar after fees and a five-percentage-point probability deduction. The full amount is targeted. Any remainder comes from fees, whole-unit purchase sizes, or limited quoted availability. This allocation rule is a heuristic.

Amounts include estimated taker fees, rounded up to a cent, and are capped to available quantity. Quotes older than 24 hours disable suggestions. Correlations between games are not modeled. Fee schedule

Reading the comparison

The market percentage comes from the price available to buy that team’s win outcome. A price of 60¢ appears as 60%. The difference is measured in percentage points. A model estimate of 70% against a 60% purchase-price percentage gives +10 percentage points.

These comparisons exclude fees. Purchase-price percentages for both teams can total more than 100% because of the bid–ask spread. The model’s probability calibration remains unverified, so a positive difference is an estimated disagreement with the market.

The snapshot carries its retrieval time. Games past scheduled tipoff are excluded. Basketball history is current through November 19, 2026. Active forecasts use fixed point-margin trees trained through June 17, 2024 and logistic calibration through June 22, 2025. Market information is excluded from model inputs, training, and calibration.
